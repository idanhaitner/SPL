# Study Flow Spec

The flow every page follows. It comes from the High Performance Systems Programming course site. Only its flow is reused; its look is not. `DESIGN.md` owns the look.

## Hierarchy

Course > Part > Chapter > section, and Chapter > Guided session > step.

- A chapter teaches. A session teaches nothing new: it makes the student use what the chapter taught.
- Step kinds: **question** (one problem with its checks) and **exercise** (C++ written and run on the page).

## Learner journey

1. Home shows the whole course and one button: start, or continue where you stopped.
2. The student reads a chapter section by section, with definitions and quick checks beside the idea they test.
3. At the end of the chapter, "Finish reading" records it as read and hands off to the session.
4. The session intro says what it covers and links back to the chapter.
5. One step at a time. Next unlocks on a correct answer or passing tests. "Skip for now" is always there.
6. Every step has a Reread link to its chapter. Hints open one at a time. Code is saved as it is typed.
7. The finish screen counts done and skipped, offers skipped steps, and leads to the next chapter.
8. Home's button returns the student to the exact place: a chapter, or a session step.

## Keep

Read then practice as two named stages; one step at a time; earned Next with a guilt-free Skip; a clickable step stem with done, skipped, current, and upcoming; a Reread link per step; resuming by step id, not step number; the last section handing off to the session; checks next to the idea.

## Improve on the reference

- Home resumes sessions, not only chapters.
- Progress on home includes session steps.
- The finish screen leads to the next chapter.
- Skipped steps are visible outside the session.

## Drop

The reference's visual language; a reading step counting as done because it loaded; marketing-style landing sections.

## Progress states

- **Ahead:** not opened.
- **Here:** where the home button leads.
- **Read:** the student pressed Finish reading.
- **Done:** read, and every session step is solved. A chapter with no session is done once read.
